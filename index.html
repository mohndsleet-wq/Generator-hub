ENGINEERING THINKING

Think like an Application Engineer.

The goal is not only to know the answer. The goal is to know what questions you should ask.

CLIENT REQUIREMENT

1000 kVA Diesel Generator

Rating Standby
Voltage 400 / 230 V
Frequency 60 Hz
Ambient 50°C
Fuel Autonomy 8 Hours

What should I think about?

01 Is 1000 kVA required at Standby or Prime rating?
02 Can the generator deliver this output at 50°C?
03 What engine and alternator combination is suitable?
04 Is the radiator suitable for the project ambient?
05 What is the full-load fuel consumption?
06 What tank capacity is required for 8 hours?
07 What ATS rating and pole configuration are required?
08 Which project standards and accessories are mandatory?

LESSONS LEARNED

Experience comes from understanding mistakes.

Every project, comment and mistake should become a permanent lesson for the next engineer.

LESSON 001

Prime vs Standby

Never select a generator only by looking at one kVA number. Always verify the required operating rating.

LESSON 002

Ambient Temperature

A generator selection is incomplete until project temperature and derating conditions are checked.

LESSON 003

Read the Specification

Small requirements such as ATS poles, noise, fuel autonomy or standards can change the entire offer.
